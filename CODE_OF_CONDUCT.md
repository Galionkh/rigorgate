# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Be honest about evidence, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate market manipulation, spam, and publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security or contact channels. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
